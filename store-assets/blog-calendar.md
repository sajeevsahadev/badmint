# Badminton 360 — 6‑month blog calendar (2 posts/week, Tue & Fri)

**How it works:** each post is written ahead of time and stamped with a `publish_at`
date. The blog + SEO prerender only show posts once that time passes (enforced in the
database), so posts publish themselves — no weekly job can fail. A twice‑weekly
Vercel rebuild (see `blog-auto-rebuild.md`) refreshes the static SEO HTML + sitemap.

**Tournament note:** *previews* are pre‑written and scheduled. *Recaps* need real
results, so they are written closer to the date (flagged 📝 below).
